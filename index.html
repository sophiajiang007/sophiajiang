THE PITCH

What came first, the lemon or the rose?

I'm Sophia, a singer-songwriter who wrote a 17-song album trying to answer this question.

It may not even make sense to you yet, but this is exactly what I bring to market — I am not simply trying to write songs. I am building a world with sound.

Anyone can write and produce a song today, but not everyone can take a lemon, a rose, the number 7, a cloud, a city, a person — and turn them into pop mythology.

So, if you want to know the answer to my question, you're going to have to start with my demos.
