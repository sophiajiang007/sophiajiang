THE PITCH

Start
here.

I'm Sophia Jiang, a singer-songwriter and producer making alternative pop for listeners drawn to emotionally detailed songs, strong sense of place, and story.

Lemons & Roses is a 17-song album I've written and independently demo-produced — the foundation of a larger artist world built through sound.

The songs move through growing up, first love, heartbreak, luck, California, Vienna, cities, people, and the strange little symbols that turn ordinary life into something bigger.

I am looking for a label partner to help develop and release Lemons & Roses and take the project from independently built demos into its next stage.
